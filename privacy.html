Privacy Policy - Beat Yesterday

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Beat Yesterday ("the game") is an offline fighting game. This page explains what information is used when you play, and why.

Information we do not collect

We do not run our own servers and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or files. Your progress (levels, heroes, coins, settings, battle pass) is saved only on your device and is deleted if you uninstall the game.

Google Play Games (optional sign-in)

You can sign in with Google Play Games. If you do, Google Play Games Services provides the game with your Play Games player ID and display name, records your achievements and leaderboard scores (highest rank, highest damage, boss damage), and keeps a copy of your game progress in your Google account (Saved Games, stored in your Google Drive app data) so you can restore it on another phone. Your leaderboard name and score can be seen by other players according to your Play Games profile settings. This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive your email or password. You can play without signing in, and you can manage or delete your Play Games data from the Google Play Games settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show optional rewarded video ads (for example to double the points you earned or to try a hero). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, general device and app information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Learn more in Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

The Elite Battle Pass can be bought through Google Play Billing. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card or payment details. Google Play tells the game only whether the purchase is completed, so it can unlock the pass (also after a reinstall). See policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and deletion

We do not sell or share personal data. Because we store nothing on our own servers, there is nothing for us to delete; uninstalling the game removes all game data from your device. Data collected by Google AdMob and Google Play is handled under Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
